About Greenwood Public School

Founded on principles of educational excellence and moral discipline, Greenwood Public School provides premier CBSE schooling from kindergarten through senior secondary level. Our campus integrates green spaces with intelligent learning infrastructure.

Our Mission

To empower young learners with rigorous intellectual stamina, ethical resilience, and global competence so they emerge as empathetic leaders of tomorrow.

Our Vision

To remain a pioneer in experiential schooling, setting benchmarks in technology-driven pedagogy, inclusivity, and holistic student achievement.

Academic Framework

Structured CBSE curriculum tailored for continuous scholastic evaluation.

FOUNDATION

Primary Wing (Grades 1-5)

DEVELOPMENT

Middle Wing (Grades 6-8)

ADVANCED

Senior Secondary (Grades 9-12)
